**New finding from the fixes (mine):** a rule-level outcome screen cannot block harm that flows through OTHER, overlapping rules. In the hard world CCL leaves 1.7-1.9% harmful approvals versus about 0% for a case-level veto. This is a design limitation of the rule-level screen, not a bug.
